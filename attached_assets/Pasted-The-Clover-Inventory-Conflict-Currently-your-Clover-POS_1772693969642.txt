The Clover Inventory Conflict
Currently, your Clover POS sees one product (e.g., "GeekVape B Coils") as a single unit. If the website sells a "Full Pack of 5," and we simply send a "1 Pack" order to Clover, Clover will only deduct one unit from your inventory instead of five.

To fix this, we need to modify Task T004 and Task T008 to include "Inventory Multiplier" logic.

The "Master Architect" Critique of the Plan:
1. The Inventory Fix (Most Important):
The plan currently calculates the price for the pack, but it doesn't tell the system how many units to deduct from Clover.

The Solution: We must instruct the Agent that when a purchaseType: "pack" is sold, the order sent to the backend must register as quantity * packSize for inventory purposes. If they buy 1 pack of 5, Clover needs to receive a "Quantity: 5" update.

2. The "Each" Labeling (T005):
The plan correctly identifies adding "each" next to the price. This is vital for customer clarity so they don't think $5 is for the whole box.

3. Database & Admin (T001-T003):
The setup for allowPackToggle and packDiscountPercent is perfect. Keeping these as "web-only" fields ensures that your Clover database doesn't get cluttered with website-specific settings.
